💡 Today's Key Points

  • Broad market: A day after the post-midterm rally, QQQ fell 6.49% and SPY 4.41%. Yahoo Finance headlines cite a Dow drop of 1,800 points and the Nasdaq entering correction.
  • Why it fell: Headlines from Reuters and WSJ point to fears of AI regulation and a data-center backlash after the Democratic win. Investing.com and IBD also cite disappointing Qualcomm and Arm reports. WSJ put the tech rout at $2.5 trillion.
  • Chips: SMH -11.21%, MU -17.46%, AMD -17.26%, ARM -13.08%, QCOM -9.49%, NVDA -9.26%. MCHP fell 13.92% before its own after-close report.
  • Big Tech: MSFT -3.58%, GOOGL -2.20%, AMZN -2.19% and AAPL -1.53% fell far less than chips.
  • Hot sectors: None rose. Consumer Defensive and Utilities held up best, while Technology and Industrials took the heaviest losses.
  • Volume surges: Almost all of the top names moved on Q3 earnings, led by healthcare and small- to mid-caps away from the AI trade.
  • Earnings highlights: U +14.93% on a big beat and its first GAAP profit. EPAM +20.63% stood out as a technology gainer.
  • After-hours focus: ABNB +11.12% is the exception. AXON, TEAM, NTRA and GH are each down more than 15%, and three of those four show an EPS beat or a revenue beat.
  • Bonds and gold: TLT +0.21% and GLD +0.81% rose modestly, well short of the scale of the equity selloff. No specific driver was confirmed from the fetched sources.

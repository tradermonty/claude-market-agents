Market Context & Read-Through

Friday's tape is an AI relief rally. Amazon's AWS grew 37% — its fastest in five years — with total revenue topping $200B and a backlog near $500B, and the read-through swept global markets: Samsung and SK Hynix rose nearly 30%, and US memory names gapped with them. Apple went the other way, down roughly 7% pre-market on a weak outlook. The market is paying for cloud capacity and selling devices.

The energy complex is the day's other story. Chevron posted its highest profit in six years and highest revenue in four as the Iran conflict lifted crude and refining margins, while Exxon missed on the same tape. Worth holding in mind that the chip sector, Amazon bounce notwithstanding, is still tracking its worst month since 2002.

No stock reached A-grade or B-grade today — the screen returned 13 names and every one scored C or D. The binding constraint is volume: 11 of the 13 show a 20-day/60-day volume ratio below 90%, meaning these gaps are being made into contracting rather than expanding participation. Only NVT (101.1%) and MPWR (99.3%) show volume holding up.

The day's biggest headline is also one of its worst setups. Amazon gapped +10.99% on an outstanding quarter and scores 53 — D-grade. The framework penalises it on three inputs at once: a gap above 10% (28% historical base rate), a negative 20-session trend into the print (-2.57%), and contracting volume. This is the system working as designed, not a verdict on the company. The same logic downgrades MPWR (+11.84% gap) and NVT (+10.66%).

Methodology & Data Provenance

Stage 1 — Universe from finviz:earnings_trading_screener() (fixed filters: market cap $10B+, earnings yesterday after-hours or today pre-market, upward EPS revision, net margin 3%+, avg volume 200k+, price $30+, upward price action). Returned 13 names; no tickers were added from any other source.

Stage 2 — Moving-average positions and current prices from finviz:get_moving_average_position, with EPS/revenue surprise and RSI from finviz:get_multiple_stocks_fundamentals. Sixty-two daily bars per name from alpaca:get_stock_bars (1 May – 30 July 2026) supplied the volume ratio (20-day average / 60-day average) and the 20-session pre-earnings trend. All MCP calls returned data for all 13 tickers; no values are estimated or interpolated.

Gap definition — Gap = pre-market price against the verified 30 July close from Alpaca. The pre-earnings trend is measured from the 1 July close to the 30 July close, so the earnings gap itself is excluded from the trend input.

Stage 3 — Weighted score = Gap Size (25%) + Pre-Earnings Trend (30%) + Volume Trend (20%) + MA200 (15%) + MA50 (10%), each scored 1–5, total normalised to 100. Grades: A ≥85, B 70–84, C 55–69, D ≤54.

Stage 4 — Every catalyst cited was verified against finviz:get_stock_news output dated 30–31 July 2026. No catalyst is inferred or assumed; where a move has no identifiable driver beyond the earnings release itself, the card says so.

Timing caveat — Prices were captured at 09:15 ET, before the US open. Pre-market gaps frequently compress at the opening auction, particularly on the largest movers.

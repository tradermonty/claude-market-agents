Volume ratio uses Alpaca daily bars from 7/29 to 10/21: the 20-day average divided by the 60-day average. The 9/18 quad-witching volume spike falls inside the 60-day window. News comes from Finviz headlines; success probabilities are analyst judgment, not model output.

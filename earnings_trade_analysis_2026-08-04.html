Stage 3 — 5-element weighted score. Gap 25% / Pre-Earnings Trend 30% / Volume 20% / MA200 15% / MA50 10%. Each element scored 1-5, then Total = (Σ(weight×points) ÷ 5) × 100. Full arithmetic is printed in each stock card.
Stage 4 — news from finviz:get_stock_news (2026-08-02 to 2026-08-04). Every stated catalyst is attributed to a specific fetched headline or to a screener/fundamental field.
Definitions — Gap = (current pre-market price ÷ 2026-08-03 close) - 1. 20d pre-earnings trend = (2026-08-03 close ÷ 2026-07-06 close) - 1. Volume ratio = (mean volume of last 20 bars) ÷ (mean volume of last 60 bars). MA readings are price as a % of the SMA. Success probability is a band-anchored linear interpolation inside the rubric's stated win-rate bands (A 70-85%, B 55-69%, C 40-54%, D 25-39%).

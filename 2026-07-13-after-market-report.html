📋 Today's Key Points
Broad Market Trend
Stocks fell broadly to start the week after the U.S. and Iran escalated fighting in the Middle East over the weekend. SPY (-0.77%), QQQ (-1.90%), DIA (-0.25%) and IWM (-0.85%) all closed lower. Breadth held up reasonably well — 722 stocks remained in confirmed uptrends — while volume-surge activity stayed thin at just 16 names, consistent with a broad risk-off repricing rather than concentrated panic selling.
Hot Sectors
Energy (+2.74%) was the standout, cushioned by crude spiking on fears of Strait of Hormuz disruption (WTI +4.4% to ~$74.62, Brent +4.3% to ~$79.31). Real Estate (+0.45%), Consumer Defensive (+0.40%) and Utilities (+0.31%) also outperformed in a defensive rotation. Technology (-2.39%) was the clear laggard as chip and AI names sold off alongside steep declines in SK Hynix and Samsung Electronics overseas, with Industrials (-1.43%) also weak.
Notable Mover: Biogen
BIIB (+4.96%, $209.03) was a standout among larger names after Truist upgraded the stock to Buy on Alzheimer's pipeline optimism ahead of key data, and the FDA cleared its Alzheimer's treatment for at-home use. Multiple analysts raised price targets following the news.

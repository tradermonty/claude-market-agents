Thu, Nov 26, 2026 · Recap of Wed, Nov 25
US markets were closed today for Thanksgiving. Friday is a half day, closing at 1:00 PM ET.

Stocks fell sharply on Wednesday morning, then won back most of the loss by the close.

📉 A deep dip, then a recovery

Intraday = during the trading day. The tall bars show how far each fund was down at its worst moment; the short bars show where it finished.

📊 The four big index funds

Index = a basket of stocks used to measure the market. These ETFs (funds that trade like a stock) track them.

  • SPY · S&P 500, about 500 large US companies
  • QQQ · Nasdaq 100, tech-heavy large companies
  • DIA · Dow Jones, 30 well-known blue-chip companies
  • IWM · Russell 2000, smaller companies ("small caps")

Large tech-heavy funds ended lower; the Dow and small caps ended higher.

🛡️ Bonds and gold both rose

  • TLT · long-term US Treasury bonds (20+ years)
  • GLD · gold

The report calls both moves consistent with a "risk-off" morning, meaning investors moved toward assets seen as safer.

🏭 Sectors: materials up, tech down

Sector = a group of companies in the same line of business.

Technology was the weakest sector of the day.

🔥 Notable movers

Earnings = a company's quarterly report of sales and profit. EPS = profit per share.

  • $OTLY Oatly · no verified reason found in the report

💡 The takeaway

A sharp morning drop was mostly erased by the close. Tech lagged, while materials, industrials, small caps, bonds and gold rose. Next week, $MRVL and $CRDO (Tue) and $CRM and $SNOW (Wed) report earnings, which the report frames as a test for the weakened AI trade.

Methodology & Data Sources

Stage 1 — Screening. finviz:earnings_trading_screener() with its fixed filter set (cap_largeover, earningsdate_yesterdayafter|todaybefore, fa_epsrev_ep, fa_netmargin_3to, sh_avgvol_o200, sh_price_o30, ta_change_u, ta_perf_0to-4w, ft=4, o=-epssurprise, ar=60). Returned 20 tickers. No tickers were added from any other source.

Stage 2 — Data acquisition. Moving-average positions from finviz:get_moving_average_position() (one call per ticker; the Price shown on each card is that call's Current Price). Daily bars from alpaca:get_stock_bars(timeframe="1Day", start="2026-05-08", end="2026-08-05"), which returns exactly 60 trading days ending at the 2026-08-04 close. Fundamentals, EPS/revenue surprise, RSI and weekly volatility from finviz:get_multiple_stocks_fundamentals(). News from finviz:get_stock_news(); ONC returned no Finviz headlines so its earnings detail was verified by web search instead, and that is stated on its card.

Stage 3 — Scoring. Gap % = (current price − 2026-08-04 close) ÷ 2026-08-04 close. Pre-earnings 20-day trend = (2026-08-04 close − 2026-07-07 close) ÷ 2026-07-07 close, i.e. measured to the last close BEFORE the earnings reaction so the gap itself is not double-counted. Volume ratio = mean(last 20 bars) ÷ mean(all 60 bars). MA200/MA50 ratios = 100 + the Finviz deviation percentage. Weighted sum = Gap×0.25 + PreEarnings×0.30 + Volume×0.20 + MA200×0.15 + MA50×0.10, then ×20 for the 0-100 score. All arithmetic was computed in Python, not estimated.

Grade bands. A = 85+ (70%+ historical win rate) · B = 70-84 (55-69%) · C = 55-69 (40-54%) · D = ≤54 (below 40%). Success probability is anchored to the midpoint mapping of these bands, not independently modelled.

Known limitations. Prices are intraday snapshots taken during the 2026-08-05 US cash session and will move. Gap percentages for names that reported yesterday after-hours reflect the current cash price versus the 2026-08-04 close, so they can be much smaller than the initial after-hours reaction (ANET is the clearest example: roughly +13% after-hours, about +2.6% by the time of this snapshot). The scoring rubric is a historical-frequency model and carries no forward guarantee.

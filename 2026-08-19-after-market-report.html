💡 Today's Key Points

  • 🧬 A single drug readout drove the entire tape Merck and Moderna announced that the Phase 3 INTerpath-001 trial of intismeran autogene plus KEYTRUDA met both recurrence-free and distant metastasis-free survival endpoints in completely resected Stage IIB-IV melanoma. MRNA closed +176.97% on 186.75M shares — roughly 43x its prior session volume — and press coverage flagged it as potentially the first S&P 500 stock since 2008 to double in a single day. MRK, a mega-cap co-developer, added +12.60%.
  • 🔥 Volume-surge characteristics: one theme, many names 80 stocks cleared the volume-surge filter at an average 2.5x relative volume and +9.8% average move. The list is unusually concentrated: the mRNA/genomics complex (TEM +24.09%, TWST +22.64%, BNTX +21.96%, CRSP +12.91%) and the precious-metals miners (HL +14.43%, AEM +11.12%, WPM +11.10%, KGC +10.25%) account for most of the top of the board. This is theme-driven breadth, not broad-market breadth.
  • 💰 Earnings highlights — all before the open Estée Lauder was the standout at +16.30% on a Q4 beat, raised margin guidance and an FY2027 profit forecast above estimates. Target added +4.28% after lifting its full-year outlook on +5.3% sales and +3% comps, aided by a $994M tariff refund — and did so via a sharp intraday reversal from a $147.80 opening gap down. On the other side, Viking Holdings fell -7.65% and TJX -4.21%.
  • 🌙 After-hours focal points: an empty board The after-hours earnings screener returned no matches on two checks after the close. There are no post-close reporters to trade tonight; the next after-close release of size is Ross Stores on Thursday 8/20. Attention shifts instead to Thursday's before-open slate, headlined by Deere.
  • 📉 Broad market: index-level calm hiding a violent rotation SPY +0.21%, DIA +0.26% and IWM +0.50% all finished slightly higher while QQQ slipped -0.18%. Beneath that, Basic Materials (+3.64%) and Healthcare (+3.38%) ran hard while Industrials (-1.19%), Financial (-0.88%) and Technology (-0.68%) funded the move. The index prints understate how much money changed hands between sectors today.
  • 🏦 Bonds & gold: the Treasury stepped in The Treasury doubled its bond buybacks, which halted the recent rout in long duration and pushed TLT +1.67% on 50.69M shares — roughly double its prior session. Gold went up alongside bonds rather than against them: GLD +3.84%, its strongest session in the recent stretch, dragging the whole mining complex with it. Bonds and gold rallying together on an intervention headline is the day's most important macro signal.

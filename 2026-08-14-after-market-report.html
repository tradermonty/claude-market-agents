💡 Today's Key Points

  • Broad market: a genuinely flat tape at the index level — SPY -0.20%, QQQ -0.14%, DIA -0.20% — after Thursday's record close. The session was consolidation, not distribution.
  • Small caps led: IWM +0.51% was the only major index green, the classic shape of a day where money rotates down the cap scale rather than out of equities.
  • Hot sectors: Energy +1.18% and Basic Materials +0.60% took the top spots, with Utilities +0.34% and Industrials +0.24% behind them. Technology -0.46% and Healthcare -0.48% were the weakest at the cap-weighted level.
  • Volume-surge character: only 35 names cleared the surge filter, but their average move was +10.1% on 2.7x relative volume — a narrow, high-conviction set rather than a broad chase.
  • Earnings highlights: Wednesday-evening reporters carried the tape. ETON +44.26% on a 126.6% EPS surprise and raised guidance, HTFL +35.70% on 48% revenue growth and a FY26 guidance raise, NMAX +14.44% on its first profitable quarter, JCAP +11.06% on record deployments.
  • Thematic flows: drone names moved on new U.S. tariffs targeting foreign drones (UMAC +24.58%), the optics complex stayed bid on AI interconnect demand (AAOI +15.53%, LPTH +15.63%), and RDDT +12.56% priced in S&P 500 inclusion effective Aug 18.
  • Bonds & gold: TLT -0.67% marked another leg lower at the long end, with yields pushing up into the weekend. GLD +0.63% held firm — the two moving in opposite directions is a real-rate story, not a risk-off one.
  • Healthcare's split personality: the sector printed -0.48% on a cap-weighted basis while four of the ten biggest surge names — ETON, HTFL, SGMT, CLYM — were healthcare. Large-cap pharma dragged; small-cap biotech and medtech had one of their best days of the month.

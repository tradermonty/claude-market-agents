⚠ Setup risk — read before sizing

Three specific hazards. (1) Overhead 200D at $47.25 — a +9% gap that halts under a declining long-term average is the classic gap-fill setup; the first close above $47.25 on expanding volume is the confirmation this score does not yet have. (2) The beat is not organic — same-hotel RevPAR -3.0% and occupancy -1.2pt mean revenue growth is unit-driven; if China lodging demand stays soft, the guidance raise gets tested next quarter. (3) ADR/China-policy overhang — HTHT is a Cayman-domiciled ADR with a beta of 0.11 to the US tape, so it does not trade with the S&P; headline risk out of China dominates and does not respect US technical levels. Pre-market prints are thin (172 shares in the 09:01 minute bar); the opening auction can reprice this materially.

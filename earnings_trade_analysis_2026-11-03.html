Methodology

Score = (Gap×0.25 + Pre-Earnings Trend×0.30 + Volume×0.20 + MA200×0.15 + MA50×0.10) × 20, each element scored 1–5. Grades: A ≥85, B 70–84, C 55–69, D ≤54. Gap = pre-market change vs 11/2 close (Finviz). MA positions: Finviz get_moving_average_position. Volume ratio = 20-day avg / 60-day avg (Alpaca daily bars through 11/2). Pre-earnings trend = 11/2 close vs close 20 trading days earlier (10/5). News analysis is based on Finviz headlines; upside probabilities are judgment estimates, not model outputs. Pre-market quotes are thin and can change by the open.

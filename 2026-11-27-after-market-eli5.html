Friday, Nov 27, 2026 · US market close

Stocks rose across the board in a short Black Friday session, led by the tech-heavy Nasdaq 100.

The market closed early, at 1:00 PM ET. Headlines described it as a rebound in AI stocks.

📈 The big indexes all rose

Index = a basket of stocks that tracks one slice of the market. Each ticker below is an ETF, a fund you can trade like one stock that holds the whole basket.
  • QQQ tracks the Nasdaq 100: 100 large, tech-heavy Nasdaq companies.
  • IWM tracks the Russell 2000: smaller US companies ("small caps").
  • SPY tracks the S&P 500: the 500 biggest US companies.
  • DIA tracks the Dow Jones: 30 large, well-known companies.

🏦 Bonds fell, gold rose

TLT holds long-term US Treasury bonds, loans to the government of 20+ years. GLD holds gold.

The report notes long-term yields rose. Gold and stocks rallied together.

🔥 Notable movers

Volume surge = a stock traded far more shares than usual. Only 12 stocks qualified on this thin holiday day.
  • $AYA Aya Gold & Silver, a silver miner. The day's biggest mover.
  • $LFTO Liftoff Mobile, an ad-tech company.
  • $KNTK Kinetik Holdings, a midstream company.
  • $DE extended its rise after its Wed 11/25 earnings report, which missed on EPS (profit per share) and beat on revenue. Earnings = a company's quarterly results.

📊 Sectors: all 11 finished higher

Sector = a group of companies in the same line of business.

The report ties Basic Materials' lead to gold strength. "Defensive" sectors, the steady everyday businesses, lagged.

💡 The takeaway

A broad, tech-led rally on a short holiday day: every sector rose, and gold climbed alongside stocks while long-term bonds fell.

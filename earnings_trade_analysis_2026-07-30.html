Market Context & Read-Through

The Fed held rates on 29 July and the reaction was hostile: the Dow posted its worst session in over a year, the Nasdaq entered correction territory, and the 30-year Treasury yield pushed toward levels last seen in 2007. Thursday's tape is a relief bounce inside that stress, not a resumption of trend.

Big-tech earnings split the AI trade cleanly — Microsoft gapped roughly 14% as Azure crossed $100B annualised, while Meta fell about 9% on ballooning AI expense. Capital is visibly rotating toward names with demonstrable AI monetisation and toward defensive cash-flow compounders such as insurance brokers.

No stock scored A-grade today. The binding constraint across the cohort is the volume element: 24 of 34 names show a 20-day/60-day volume ratio below 100%, meaning these gaps are being made on contracting rather than expanding participation. That is a poor backdrop for gap continuation, and the framework is correctly refusing to award top grades. Position sizing should reflect that.

Methodology & Data Provenance

Stage 1 — Universe from finviz:earnings_trading_screener() (fixed filters: market cap $10B+, earnings yesterday after-hours or today pre-market, upward EPS revision, net margin 3%+, avg volume 200k+, price $30+, upward price action). Returned 34 names; no tickers were added from other sources.

Stage 2 — Moving-average positions from finviz:get_moving_average_position and Finviz fundamentals; 60 daily bars per name from alpaca:get_stock_bars (1 May – 29 July 2026) for the volume ratio (20-day average / 60-day average) and the 20-session pre-earnings trend measured to the 29 July close, so the earnings gap itself is excluded from the trend input.

Stage 3 — Weighted score = Gap Size (25%) + Pre-Earnings Trend (30%) + Volume Trend (20%) + MA200 (15%) + MA50 (10%), each scored 1–5, total normalised to 100. Grades: A ≥85, B 70–84, C 55–69, D ≤54.

Verification — Gap sizes were computed from the intraday price against the verified 29 July close and independently reconciled against Finviz's 1-day performance field for every name. Two initial data batches were discarded after failing a duplicate-value integrity check and were re-run against live quotes before use.

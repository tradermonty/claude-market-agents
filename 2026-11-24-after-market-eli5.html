Tuesday, November 24, 2026 · US market close

Tech stocks fell while healthcare, retailers and the Dow rose.

📈 The big indexes split

Index = a basket of stocks used to measure the market. Each ticker below is a fund (ETF) that tracks one index.
  • S&P 500: 500 large US companies, the broad market.
  • Nasdaq 100: large Nasdaq-listed companies, heavy in tech.
  • Dow Jones: 30 large, long-established US companies.
  • Russell 2000: smaller US companies ("small caps").

The tech-heavy Nasdaq 100 fell most. The Dow and small caps still rose.

🏢 Healthcare up, technology down

Sector = a group of companies in the same line of business.

Top three and bottom three sectors of the day. Healthcare was the best, technology the worst.

🔥 Stocks that moved on news

Earnings = a company's quarterly results. "Outlook" or "guidance" = its own forecast for coming quarters.
  • $ANF: quarterly results beat expectations; outlook raised.
  • $KSS: same-store sales back to growth; full-year outlook raised.
  • $INSM: Sanofi agreed to buy it for $41B in cash.
  • $ADI: next-quarter guidance came in below expectations.

🌙 After the closing bell

After-hours trading = trading after the 4 p.m. ET close, often when companies report earnings. Volume is thin, so early prices can swing.

Software and retail companies that reported after the close traded lower in early prints. These prices are preliminary.

🏦 Bonds and gold slipped

  • $TLT: a fund holding US government bonds (Treasuries) maturing in 20+ years.
  • $GLD: a fund that tracks the price of gold.

Gold gave back part of Monday's +3.15% rally.

💡 The takeaway

Money rotated between groups rather than leaving stocks: tech fell, but healthcare, retailers, the Dow and small caps rose.

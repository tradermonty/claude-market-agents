Read of the day. The screener returned 16 large-cap names that beat on EPS and are gapping up — half again as many as yesterday's 11 — and exactly one, MANH (85.0), cleared the A-grade line, with GRMN (70.0) the only B. The remaining fourteen cluster tightly between 53 and 67, which is the signature of a broad, undifferentiated beat day: plenty of good quarters, very few good setups. The binding constraint is again the 30%-weighted pre-earnings trend. Four names (IEX -0.58%, JCI -0.14%, GEHC -1.45%, TEVA -4.92%) are printing beats into flat or falling 20-day trends, and the model treats those as mean-reversion candidates rather than momentum continuations.

The instructive contrast today is headline surprise versus market reaction. WPC posted the largest EPS surprise on the board at +64.56% and the market moved it just +1.16%; AER beat by +45.09% and gapped +0.66%. Both are net-lease/leasing businesses where headline EPS is distorted by gain-on-sale and impairment timing, and the muted response says participants know it. At the other end, MANH beat by only +5.46% but gapped +12.39% — the market is repricing record cloud growth, not the EPS line. Note that MANH's gap lands in the 10%+ bucket (28% historical win rate), which is why its success estimate is held at 66% rather than the A-band's nominal 70%+.

Participation is the other broad weakness: twelve of the 16 names have a 20-day volume ratio below their 60-day average, with VRSK (78.3%), ACGL (78.6%) and AER (80.0%) the thinnest. Only four — MANH (111.8%), EXE (118.3%), MDLZ (105.7%) and GEHC (101.1%) — show participation genuinely rising into the print.

Methodology

Stage 1 — Screening. finviz:earnings_trading_screener() only, with its fixed filter set: large-cap ($10B+), earnings released yesterday after the close or today pre-market, upward EPS revision, net margin 3%+, average volume 200K+, price $30+, price trending up, sorted by EPS surprise descending. No results were added from any other source. The screen returned 16 stocks.

Stage 2 — Data. For every ticker: finviz:get_moving_average_position for the 20/50/200-day SMA levels; alpaca:get_stock_bars (1Day, 2026-04-27 to 2026-07-29, 64 bars returned, last bar 2026-07-28) for the volume trend, computed as the mean of the most recent 20 bars divided by the mean of the trailing 60 bars; and the same bar series for the 20-day pre-earnings trend, measured from the 2026-06-29 close to the 2026-07-28 close — the last close before the announcement, so the trend is genuinely pre-earnings. MA percentages are computed as the pre-market price divided by the SMA level, so every MA figure is consistent with the price shown on each card.

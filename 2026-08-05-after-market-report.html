💡 Today's Key Points

  • Gold is the standout. GLD closed +4.14% at $389.64 on 14.7M shares — roughly 2.6x the prior session's 5.8M. Precious-metals miners showed up across the volume-surge list (EGO, SSRM, AYA), and Basic Materials led every sector at +2.71%.
  • Indexes split, breadth soft. DIA +0.44% was the only major index green; SPY -0.20%, IWM -0.64%, and QQQ -0.90% lagged. Large-cap value held up while growth and small caps gave ground.
  • Volume surge is almost entirely earnings-driven. 83 names cleared the screen with an average move of +8.9% and 2.3x relative volume. Ten of the top twelve gainers moved on their own quarterly results; only BSP (Airtable acquisition) and CMPS (pipeline news, EPS actually missed) had a non-earnings driver.
  • After-hours reporters swept consensus. All 9 large-cap names up 2%+ after the close beat EPS estimates, averaging a +16.61% surprise. EXPE posted the largest beat at +39.31%, followed by CGNX at +36.93% and SITM at +24.44%.
  • Two reversal setups to watch. SITM fell 6.85% in the regular session and then jumped 5.87% after hours on a +24.44% EPS beat. MCK ran +5.64% into the print and added another 2.60% after hours, though its revenue came in 4.98% below consensus.
  • Bonds barely moved. TLT closed +0.22% at $83.00. The risk-off tone did not show up in long-duration Treasuries, which leaves gold's 4% move looking more like a commodity-specific bid than a broad flight to safety.
  • Communication Services took the worst of it at -2.35%, with Energy -1.99% and Industrials -1.51% also lagging. Energy weakness at the sector level did not stop FTK and WTTR from posting 20%+ earnings gains.

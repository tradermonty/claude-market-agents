Methodology

Score = (Gap×0.25 + Pre-Earnings Trend×0.30 + Volume×0.20 + MA200×0.15 + MA50×0.10) × 20, each element scored 1–5. Grades: A ≥85, B 70–84, C 55–69, D ≤54. Gap = pre-market price vs 11/3 close (Alpaca). MA ratios = report price / Finviz 50-day and 200-day SMA from get_moving_average_position. Volume ratio = 20-day avg / 60-day avg (Alpaca daily bars 8/11 through 11/3). Pre-earnings trend = 11/3 close vs close 20 trading days earlier (10/6). Average volumes are computed from values truncated to the thousand, which does not change any score bucket. News analysis is based on Finviz headlines only; article bodies and company releases were not read. Upside probabilities are judgment estimates, not model outputs. Pre-market quotes are thin and can change by the open: several prices moved during data collection, and gaps near the 2% boundary (SOLS, JKHY, SWK, GWW) can change a score by 5 points.

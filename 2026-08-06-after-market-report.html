  • 🔥The volume-surge cohort was almost entirely post-earnings. All ten of today's biggest surge names moved on a quarterly report or guidance — PaySign +29.30%, Aeva +28.81%, SiTime +26.58%, WPP +26.06%, Paycom +23.70%. Average move across the 91 surge stocks was +9.3% on 2.4x relative volume.

  • 🌙After-hours was the day's real action. Ten large caps that reported after the bell are up 2%+, led by Atlassian +26.10% (EPS surprise +31.22%, revenue +5.37%) and JFrog +24.14% (EPS +26.58%). Natera +22.95% and Airbnb +10.45% rallied on revenue strength despite negative EPS surprise readings at capture time.
  • ⚠️Read the after-hours numbers with care. Prices were captured at 16:10 ET, about ten minutes into a thin session. Airbnb released at 16:03 ET and Instacart at 16:00 ET, so their surprise percentages may not yet reflect the new quarter.
  • 🏦Bonds and gold gave no risk-off signal. TLT fell 0.58%, meaning long yields rose on the day, while GLD finished essentially unchanged at +0.01%. Neither confirms a flight to safety behind the equity drift.
  • 📈Breadth stayed constructive. 788 stocks remain in uptrends, led by Financial with 202 names, then Healthcare and Industrials at 120 each — a broader base than the index tape suggests.

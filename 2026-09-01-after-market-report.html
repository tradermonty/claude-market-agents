💡 Today's Key Points

Hot sectors

Energy +1.70% was the only sector with real momentum, with crude bid on reports of tankers struck near Hormuz. Utilities +0.67% and Healthcare +0.64% rounded out the green — a defensive top three.

Volume-surge character

36 names, average relative volume 2.2x and an average move of +5.0%. Breadth in the surge list came almost entirely from biotech/vaccines and energy/materials; not a single mega-cap technology name made the cut.

Earnings highlights

DELL, PANW, MDB, GTLB and CRDO all reported at 16:05 ET and all five beat consensus EPS. Dell was the standout with a +64.09% EPS surprise, +22.66% on revenue and a raised outlook driven by AI servers.

After-hours focal points

The reaction split despite five beats: DELL +7.53%, GTLB +5.34% and PANW +4.60% versus CRDO -6.61% and MDB -12.83%. A beat alone is not clearing the bar — the market is pricing the guide, not the quarter.

Broad market trend

September opened red across the board, with the Nasdaq (QQQ -1.27%) and small caps (IWM -1.12%) leading the decline and the S&P (-0.69%) holding up better. 451 names still screen as being in an uptrend, so this reads as rotation rather than a wholesale break.

Bonds & gold

TLT -0.79% — long yields pushed higher and weighed on equity sentiment. Gold took the harder hit: GLD -2.84%, its sharpest single-session drop in weeks, an unwind that ran alongside, not against, the equity selloff.

Methodology — backtest scoring

Each of the five factors is scored 1–5 against historical success-rate bands, converted to a 0–100 sub-score (points ÷ 5 × 100), then weighted: Gap×0.25 + PreEarnings×0.30 + Volume×0.20 + MA200×0.15 + MA50×0.10. Grades: A ≥85 (70%+ success) · B 70–84 (55–69%) · C 55–69 (40–54%) · D ≤54 (below 40%). MA position ratios are price ÷ SMA × 100. The volume ratio compares the trailing 20-session average against the trailing 60-session average, both ending at the last pre-earnings close (2026-08-24), so the earnings-day volume spike does not contaminate the baseline.

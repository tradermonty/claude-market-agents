Methodology

5-element backtest score = Gap Size×0.25 + Pre-Earnings Trend×0.30 + Volume Trend×0.20 + MA200×0.15 + MA50×0.10, on a 1–5 point scale, converted to 100. Grades: A 85+, B 70–84, C 55–69, D ≤54.

Gap = Finviz pre-market price vs the 11/11 regular-session close. Pre-earnings trend = 11/11 close vs the close 20 trading days earlier (10/14). Volume ratio = average of the last 20 daily bars ÷ average of the last 60 daily bars, through 11/11. MA positions are from get_moving_average_position and are relative to the pre-market price. Success probability is a judgment estimate, not a model output.

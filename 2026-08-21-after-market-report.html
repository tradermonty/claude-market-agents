💡 Today's Key Points

  • Materials sweep the tape. Basic Materials led every sector at +2.99%, more than double the next-best group. Copper and precious-metals names supplied most of the breadth: FCX +7.67%, ERO +9.43%, NEXA +9.38%, EGO +7.22%, AU +5.27%, WPM +4.98%, TECK +4.58%.
  • Gold does the heavy lifting. GLD closed +1.96% at $423.41, the largest move of the six major ETFs and the direct driver behind the gold-miner cluster in the volume-surge list.
  • Crypto-linked financials rip. Bitcoin is tracking its best week in over two years on U.S. regulatory momentum, and the equity proxies followed: HOOD +13.72% led the S&P 500, with CRCL +5.09% and Strive (ASST) +12.96% alongside it.
  • mRNA and oncology stay hot. MRNA added +8.86% on 7.58x relative volume and is now +129% over one week. The read-through carried ARCT to +22.38% (today's top mover) and SLS to +15.37%, the latter after an analyst note framing Moderna-Merck cancer-vaccine data as positive for the oncology group.
  • Surge breadth is broad, not narrow. 62 tickers cleared the volume-surge filter at an average +7.3% move on 2.2x relative volume, and 727 names sit in confirmed up-trends — led by Healthcare (206), Technology (98) and Energy (88).
  • Utilities are the lone real casualty. The sector fell -1.96% while Energy (-0.07%) and Real Estate (-0.04%) were effectively unchanged. Nothing else finished red.
  • Dow beats the Nasdaq. DIA +0.89% and IWM +0.76% outpaced SPY +0.40% and QQQ +0.35% — the day's leadership sat in cyclicals and small caps rather than mega-cap tech, where Technology managed only +0.15%.
  • Bonds slip as gold runs. TLT closed -0.35% at $82.05. Long-duration Treasuries and gold moved in opposite directions, so the metals bid reads as a commodity and reflation trade rather than a broad flight to safety.

💡 Today's Key Points

  • Broad risk-on close. All four major index ETFs finished green, led by the Dow (DIA +0.97%) over the Nasdaq (QQQ +0.88%) and S&P 500 (SPY +0.84%). Small caps lagged (IWM +0.41%), so the advance was carried by large-cap cyclicals rather than by breadth.
  • Hot sectors. Industrials +1.31%, Communication Services +1.30% and Technology +1.12% led; nine of eleven sectors closed higher. Only Utilities (-0.31%) and Healthcare (-0.09%) fell — a defensive-out, cyclical-in mix.
  • AI hardware was the day's single biggest theme. Dell +11.95%, HPE +12.42% and HP Inc +8.37% all surged on AI server and hardware demand, with Dell hitting an all-time high after RBC Capital initiated coverage at Outperform. The move spread down the supply chain to IT distributors Avnet (+7.16%) and Ingram Micro (+4.49%).
  • Volume-surge characteristics. Only 33 tickers cleared the surge filter — a narrow, concentrated day — but the average move was a large +7.5% at 3.0x average relative volume. Two names accounted for most of the tail: ACVA and FEIM.
  • Earnings highlights. Frequency Electronics (+42.40%) reported fiscal Q1 2027 after Thursday's close with record revenue of $23.5M, up 70% year over year, and an EPS surprise of +173.3%. Macy's (+7.71%) rebounded from Thursday's guidance-driven drop after a Q2 beat and a raised full-year profit outlook.
  • M&A, not earnings, drove the top mover. ACV Auctions jumped 44.20% on Copart's $1.9B all-cash takeover offer. Relative volume hit 29.4x — the highest on the board. Treat this as a deal-spread situation, not a momentum breakout.
  • Bonds and gold. TLT closed +0.11% and GLD +0.57%. Long-duration Treasuries barely moved even as equities rallied, with the session's news flow pointing to firmer inflation data and rising rate expectations. Gold's steady bid alongside a risk-on tape suggests inflation hedging rather than fear.

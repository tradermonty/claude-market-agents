Methodology & Data Notes

Score = (Gap×0.25 + 20-Day Trend×0.30 + Volume×0.20 + MA200×0.15 + MA50×0.10) on a 1-5 scale, converted to 100 points. Grades: A ≥85, B 70-84, C 55-69, D ≤54.

Gap = pre-market price vs 10/30 close. 20-Day Trend = 10/30 close vs close 20 trading days earlier (10/2), i.e. measured before the earnings gap. Volume trend = last 20 daily bars avg / last 60 bars avg (Alpaca, 8/7-10/30). MA positions from Finviz get_moving_average_position. EPS/revenue surprise from Finviz fundamentals. Up-probability is a qualitative estimate, not a model output. Pre-market prices can move materially by the open.

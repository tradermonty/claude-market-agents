Methodology

Stage 2 — Data: finviz:get_moving_average_position for MA deviations; alpaca:get_stock_bars (1Day, 2026-04-17 → 2026-07-15, 60 trading bars) for volume ratio (20-day avg ÷ 60-day avg) and the pre-earnings 20-day trend. Gap % is measured from the 2026-07-15 close to the current pre-market quote and cross-checks exactly against Finviz 1-day performance for all three names.

Stage 3 — Scoring: Total = (Gap×0.25 + Pre-Earnings×0.30 + Volume×0.20 + MA200×0.15 + MA50×0.10) × 20, each element scored 1–5. Grades: A ≥85 · B 70–84 · C 55–69 · D ≤54.

Stage 4 — News: finviz:get_stock_news (5-day lookback) per ticker.

Trade plans are heuristic, not from the backtest: stop = 2× daily volatility below entry, target = 2R, sizing = 1% account risk on a $100,000 reference account. Success probabilities are analyst judgment informed by the backtest bands, not measured outcomes.
